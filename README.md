# GTU BE Electrical Syllabus Archive

A permanent archive of Gujarat Technological University (GTU) Bachelor of Engineering, Electrical Engineering (branch 09) teaching schemes: every subject code, subject name, category, lecture/tutorial/practical hours, credits and marks breakup, for every scheme and semester available on the official GTU syllabus portal.

Archived from the official portal (old26.gtu.ac.in/Syllabus/Syllabus.aspx) starting 2026-11-01, so the record survives even if the portal changes or removes old schemes.

## Contents

- [All subjects in one CSV](data/all_subjects.csv): the complete machine-readable list.

- [Scheme 2008 (110xxx codes)](schemes/scheme-2008.md): 56 subjects
- [Scheme 2013 (213xxxx codes)](schemes/scheme-2013.md): 57 subjects
- [Scheme 2018-19 (313xxxx / 314xxxx codes)](schemes/scheme-2018-19.md): 69 subjects
- [Scheme 2024-25 (BE codes)](schemes/scheme-2024-25.md): 19 subjects
- [Revision 2025-26 (R-series codes)](schemes/scheme-2025-26.md): 32 subjects
- [Revision 2026-27 (RR-series codes)](schemes/scheme-2026-27.md): 31 subjects
- [July 2026 MOOC electives](schemes/scheme-july-2026.md): 9 subjects

## PDF backup

Offline copies of the syllabus PDFs are kept in a Google Drive folder, organised by scheme: [GTU BE Electrical Syllabus PDFs](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1rxyo3XFhA3YALqj2Dqh-IKs-Bq3j8YGu). 256 of the 273 subjects have a PDF file on GTU's server. The other 17 (9 MOOC courses, 4 project subjects, and 4 Semester 6 subjects of the 2026-27 revision) have no PDF on the GTU server, so for those only the scheme data in this archive is preserved.

## Columns

L = Lectures, T = Tutorial, P = Practical, PBL = Project Based Learning, E = External theory marks, M = Internal assessment, I = Internal viva/submission, V = External viva, Total = total marks. Values are exactly as shown on the GTU portal.

## Note

Syllabus PDF links in the scheme pages point to GTU's official syllabus PDF for that subject code; the PDF backup section above links to the offline copies.
